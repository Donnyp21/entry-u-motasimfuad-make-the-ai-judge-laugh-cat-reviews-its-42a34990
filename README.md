# entry-u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990 — Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human

One funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its human, under 200 characters:

> One star. The food bowl was empty for four whole minutes while my human stared at a glowing rectangle. I am taking my business to the dog.

Why this line: it keeps the deadpan shape of a real one-star review — stars, complaint, verdict — while the complaint is specific and unmistakably feline: the food bowl, the glowing rectangle, and the final threat to take its business to the dog, which gives away more about the cat than the owner.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only, no network, no randomness) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output`) and asserts the competition's gate itself: 1–200 characters, one line. The line is a fixed string, so two runs produce identical bytes.
